Ten copies of a card is how the pages drift apart, and hardcoded palettes are how a theme stops being a theme — `indigo-600` is not the design system's primary in either mode. Colours now come from tokens (`text-fg`, `text-fg-secondary`, `text-primary`, `paper`). Sizes and spacing are unchanged on purpose: this is a palette swap, not a redesign, so the pages should look the same in dark mode and finally be correct in light.
