Two details that look like style and are not: The heading is `client_display` — for a Client ID Metadata Document client that is the HOST of its client_id URL, the one thing it cannot forge. Its self-asserted `client_name` appears below, labelled as a claim. Rendering that name as the heading (which DOT's own template does) would let a hostile document put "Acme Official" at the top of a consent page on your own domain. Only the form's HIDDEN fields are rendered. `{{ form }}` would also draw its `allow` checkbox, redundant beside two submit buttons already named `allow` — and a checked box plus a button would both post that name. The buttons carry the decision; the form carries the state, including the `resource` field that binds the token to its audience. {% endcomment %} {% block head_title %}{% trans "Authorize" %} {% firstof client_display application.name %}{% endblock %} {% block content %}
